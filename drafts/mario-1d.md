## Exposition

Since I was pretty young I learnt about the theoretical possibility of 4+ dimensional reality. And I had this nagging urge for to be able to perceive it.

Now It's not something I did something about, but it was there, lurking.

## Rising Action

At one boring-ish train ride I had this bizarre thought. If we, as humans, can perceive 3D reality via 2D sensor (== our eyes), why can't we perceive 4D reality via our understanding of 3D. Or maybe we can do the same dimensional leap but instead of going 2 → 3, why not 2 → 4 (the lesser equivalent of 1→3 seemed to me pretty feasible, albeit pretty mind-numbing).

Anyway, that went nowhere, I accepted the fact I would never think in 4D.

But it dawned on me, why not go the other way around? If all 3d games are using 2 projection, why not making 2d game and make it 1d. I present to you - Mario.

Pre-AI me had tried doing this with ray casting, selecting what the ray should hit (eg mushrooms, floor, pipes), what it shouldn't (Mario, sky), how it will look and how to interact with it.

Fixing bugs. Hideous UI. Performance Issues. Dreadful code. More Bugs. Defeat.

## Climax

few months ago I did this with {Claude, Codex, Cursor} and you know it was easy, took 10 minutes and worked perfectly.

## Falling Action

I'm not sure about you, but I expected it to either be mind-blowing, light bulb moment etc OR simply unplayable. And, well, it's none of these. It's just intuitive.

It's pretty baffling, we can intuitively play 2D game in 1D, 2D in 2D and 3D in 2D. we can also conceive both 2D infographic in 2D and 3D scene in 2D and even conceive 3D in 1D given enough time.

What's going on?

## Resolution

The dimension leap is there, but none of that gets us even close to understanding 4d with our limited monkey brain.

Which makes me think the problem here is not the medium, the perception dimensions or the reality dimensions. Its us, we are just wired (or well-trained) for three dimensions
